BANTO（番頭） — in traditional Japanese merchant houses, the trusted steward who ran the business behind the scenes. BANTO is a Japan-based team supporting overseas businesses, with three services: Back Office, BD and Account. BANTO BD opens your first partner relationships in Japan.
Sound familiar?

Japan is hard to read from abroad.

  • Emails in English from HQ rarely get an answer — or get a polite reply that leads nowhere.
  • Many Japanese companies take first contact through inquiry forms and introductions, not cold email.
  • You can't tell whether the problem is the product, the positioning, or the approach.
  • A country manager or a Japan entity is a big commitment before you know Japan is worth it.

Is it the product — or the approach?

When Japanese companies don't respond, the reason isn't always the product. Often it's who you approached, through which channel, and how the first message read in Japanese.

Separating those two questions early is what tells you whether Japan really fits your product.

What we do

Find
