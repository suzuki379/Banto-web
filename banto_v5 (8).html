Our Philosophy

Why we're called BANTO.

In traditional Japanese merchant culture, the banto (番頭) was not just a manager — they were the person a business owner trusted completely. The one who knew the books, the relationships, the unspoken rules, and the long-term vision.

That role doesn't exist in modern corporate structures. But for foreign companies entering Japan, it should.

BANTO was built to fill that gap. We don't just advise. We operate alongside you, hold institutional knowledge, and make decisions you'd make yourself if you'd spent 15 years inside Japan's business ecosystem.

Why BANTO

Have you heard of Japan's
best-kept hiring secret?

Since around 2020, a new way of working has quietly taken hold in Japan — the "online secretary" model. Skilled professionals offering exactly the functions a company needs, on a flexible contract basis. Former corporate veterans who stepped back for childcare and chose independence. Highly capable. Underutilized. And until recently, invisible to foreign companies.

The missing piece was always a director — someone who could receive requirements in a foreign language and translate them into results on the ground in Japan.

BANTO's founder spent 15 years in Europe as a professional musician — leading and collaborating across cultures in orchestras where dozens of nationalities must perform as one. While still based in France, she began supporting Japanese companies with their overseas expansion online. When the pandemic accelerated remote work, she deepened her involvement in Japan's growing online professional community.

After returning to Japan, she served as an executive officer at a Japanese company — building PR, executive support, and back-office infrastructure from scratch. She went on to do the same for many companies as an external partner, while lecturing to a community of 700+ online professionals.

Then she saw the gap. Japan had the talent. Foreign companies needed it. No one was building the bridge.
